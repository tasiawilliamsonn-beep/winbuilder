Teacher Resources

Ready-made slides for the small group rotation. Pick a standard, or a day from the unit you built, then present it, print it, or save it as a PDF.

Small group slides

Each deck follows the small group lesson: learning target, big idea, notes with fill-in blanks and an answer slide, a worked example revealed one step at a time, a problem to try together, and a stop-and-jot.

Presenting: use the arrow keys, the space bar, or tap the left and right sides of the screen. Press Esc to stop. Your own unit's lessons show up here after you build a unit in the builder on this device.
