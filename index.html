WIN Portal

Build standards-based WIN rotations for grade 6 math and ELA, share them with students in Canvas, and see who needs what the next day.

🛠️
1. BuildPick standards or write your own questions, then download a student portal.
 🧭

 📊
3. AnalyzePaste turn-ins to see skill mastery, misconceptions, and next-day groups.

To edit an example, download its unit file (math, ELA), then open the builder and tap "Open unit file."

How it works

  1. In the builder, check the standards students need, generate a unit, and preview it.
  2. Tap "Download student portal" and upload that file to your Canvas course.
  3. Make a text-entry Canvas assignment for each WIN day. Students tap "Turn in my work," copy, and paste.
  4. After class, download the submissions and upload them to the dashboard.

No student data is sent to this website. Student work stays on the student's device until they paste it into Canvas, and the dashboard runs only in the teacher's browser.
