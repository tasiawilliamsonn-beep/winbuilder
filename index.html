A WIN week

Before

  • Check the dashboard's Next steps tab for groups.
  • Build or reopen a unit.
  • Post the portal and one Canvas assignment per day, or print the paper packet.

During

  • Pull your small group.
  • Others work in Independent or IXL/RACE.
  • Students tap Turn in my work and paste into Canvas.

After

  • Skim scores in SpeedGrader, or
  • choose Download Submissions and drop the zip on the dashboard.
  • Tap Copy groups for tomorrow.
